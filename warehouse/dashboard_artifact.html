Companies & energy

H1

Petrobras earns on price, not volume

Mixed
Claim
Brent explains at least 70% of the swings in Petrobras's quarterly USD revenue; adding production volume explains less than 10 points more.
Test
Regress log(quarterly revenue in USD) on log(Brent) and log(Petrobras-operated output), 2016 onward. Compare R² with and without volume.
Threshold
Supported if R²(Brent) ≥ 0.70 and volume adds < 0.10; Mixed if 0.50 ≤ R² < 0.70
Result
42 quarters 2016-Q1–2026-Q2: Brent alone explains 69% of revenue swings (elasticity 0.68); adding volume explains +3.4 pts more
Data
revenue_usd_bn[PETR], brent_usd, operated_oil_production_kbd[PETR] · as of 2026-06-30
H2

Oil exports grow by volume; iron ore doesn't

Supported
Claim
Crude-oil export tonnes grow ≥ 5%/yr since 2016 while iron-ore tonnes stay roughly flat (within ±2%/yr).
Test
Log-linear trend of 12-month rolling export tonnes (ComexStat), 2016 onward.
Threshold
Supported if oil ≥ +5%/yr and |iron ore| ≤ 2%/yr; Mixed if only one holds
Result
Oil export tonnes +8.2%/yr vs iron ore +1.0%/yr since 2016; pre-salt = 79% of oil output (Aug 2026)
Data
oil_exports_kg, iron_ore_exports_kg, presalt_share · as of 2026-08-01
H3

Vale is a China iron-ore price proxy

Supported
Claim
Vale's quarterly USD revenue moves with the iron-ore export price (correlation > 0.7) while export tonnes trend < 2%/yr.
Test
Correlation of Vale quarterly USD revenue with the quarterly average iron-ore export unit value (US$/t), 2016 onward.
Threshold
Supported if ρ > 0.7 and |tonnes trend| < 2%/yr; Mixed if 0.4 ≤ ρ; Not supported if ρ < 0.4
Result
ρ(Vale revenue, iron-ore US$/t) = 0.77 over 42 quarters; tonnes trend +1.0%/yr
Data
revenue_usd_bn[VALE], iron_ore_unit_value, iron_ore_exports_kg, china_export_share · as of 2026-06-30
H4

Droughts raise power costs more than they cut Axia's output

Mixed
Claim
In months when reservoir storage (EAR) is below 40%, the marginal cost of power (CMO) is more than double its level in other months, while the share of national generation from Axia (formerly Eletrobras, mostly hydro) falls by more than 3 points.
Test
Compare low-storage months (EAR < 40%) with the rest, 2016 onward. Axia's share is measured against its own calendar-year average, so the steady rise of rooftop solar in the national total does not bias the comparison.
Threshold
Supported if CMO ratio > 2× and share falls > 3 pts; Mixed if only one holds
Result
42 low-storage months: CMO averaged R$385/MWh vs R$124 otherwise (3.1×); Axia's share was 0.7 pts lower than its same-year average
Data
stored_energy_ear, generation_share_sin[AXIA], cmo_power_cost · as of 2026-09-01
H5

Resource champions lagged the index in dollars since 2012

Not supported
Claim
Petrobras (PETR4) and Vale (VALE3) both delivered a lower total return in US dollars (dividends reinvested) than the Ibovespa from January 2012 to today.
Test
USD total-return index, Jan 2012 = 100, for PETR4 and VALE3 vs the Ibovespa (itself a total-return index) converted to USD, compared in the same month.
Threshold
Supported if both are below the Ibovespa; Mixed if one is; Not supported if neither
Result
US$100 in Jan 2012 → Petrobras $325, Vale $140, Ibovespa $99 (Oct 2026, dividends reinvested)
Data
total_return_usd[PETR,VALE,IBOV] · as of 2026-10-31
H6

Suzano is the currency hedge the index lacks

Mixed
Claim
Suzano's monthly returns in reais correlate positively (ρ > 0.35) with the real's depreciation, while Itaú's (the domestic control) correlate negatively.
Test
Correlation of monthly total returns in BRL with the monthly % change in BRL per USD, 2012 onward.
Threshold
Supported if ρ(Suzano) > 0.35 and ρ(Itaú) < 0; Mixed if one holds
Result
ρ(Suzano, BRL weakening) = +0.18; ρ(Itaú, BRL weakening) = -0.57 over 177 months
Data
total_return_brl[SUZB,ITUB], brl_usd · as of 2026-10-02

Households & labour

L1

Debt service is squeezing shoppers

Mixed
Claim
A rise in the household debt-service ratio is followed by slower retail volumes: correlation of its 12-month change with retail-volume growth six months later is below −0.3.
Test
ρ(12-month change in debt-service ratio at t, retail volume growth year on year at t+6), 2012 onward.
Threshold
Supported if ρ < −0.3; Mixed if −0.3 ≤ ρ < 0; Not supported if ρ ≥ 0
Result
ρ = -0.26 over 169 months; debt service now 28.7% of income (series high 28.7%, Jul 2026)
Data
household_debt_service_ratio, retail_sales_volume · as of 2026-07-01
L2

Formal hiring warns before unemployment turns

Supported
Claim
Formal net hiring (CAGED, the labour ministry's register of formal jobs) leads unemployment: its 12-month average correlates below −0.4 with the change in the unemployment rate over the following 12 months.
Test
ρ(CAGED 12-month average at t, unemployment(t+12) − unemployment(t)), 2021 onward. 12-month windows remove the seasonality of both unadjusted series; 2020 is excluded as the COVID shock. (Revised on review from a 3-month / 6-month version that leaned on the 2020 months.)
Threshold
Supported if ρ < −0.4; Mixed if −0.4 ≤ ρ < 0; Not supported if ρ ≥ 0
Result
ρ = -0.44 over 56 months; latest CAGED +166k jobs (Aug 2026), unemployment 5.3%
Data
caged_net_hires, unemployment_rate · as of 2026-08-01

Public finances

L3

Interest outruns growth on public debt

Supported
Claim
The implicit interest rate on public debt exceeds nominal GDP growth by more than 3 points, so debt/GDP rises even with a balanced primary budget.
Test
r = the public sector's net interest bill over 12 months (BCB fiscal data) ÷ gross government debt a year earlier, both in reais; g = 12-month nominal GDP growth. Nominal, so not the same number as the real-rate spread in the 2012-book section.
Threshold
Supported if r − g > 3 pts; Mixed if 0 < r − g ≤ 3; Not supported if ≤ 0
Result
Aug 2026: interest rate on debt 12.3% vs nominal growth 7.2% → r − g = +5.1 pts
Data
interest_bill_gdp, gross_public_debt_gdp, gdp_nominal_12m_brl · as of 2026-08-01

L4

Exports pivot from the US to China

Supported
Claim
Over the last 12 months, exports to the US fell while exports to China rose, versus the prior 12 months.
Test
Sum of monthly FOB exports, last 12 months vs the 12 before, by destination.
Threshold
Supported if US change < 0 and China change > 0; Mixed if one holds
Result
12 months to Aug 2026 vs prior 12: US -14.3%, China +19.0%, EU +12.1%

exports_to_us, exports_to_china, exports_to_eu · as of 2026-08-01

The 2012 book, checked

James Dale Davidson's Brazil Is the New America (2012) made a set of bets. The evidence for each, computed from the same warehouse.

  1. Hyperinflation as inoculation

    CPI 1994=2,076% -> 2023=4.6%

  2. Commodity supercycle or structural rise

    avg GDP growth 2004-2010=4.50% vs 2015-2023=0.55%

  3. External financing quality

    CA=-1.00%GDP, FDI=2.95%GDP (2023); FDI/|CA|=2.95

  4. Fiscal sustainability / structurally high real rates

    gross debt 83%GDP, interest bill 8.9%GDP, primary -0.6%GDP; real rate 9.1% vs growth 1.4% -> stabilizing gap +6.9pts

  5. Commodity export concentration

    soy+oil+iron-ore = 34% of exports over the 12 months to 2026-08

  6. Pre-salt oil — Rio is the new Houston

    oil output 4,601 kbbl/d (2026-07), ~2.2x the 2010 average (2,137)

  7. Brazil vs China — demand dependence

    China = 29% of exports (2024+ avg), up from 18% in 2014; niobium ~US$305M/mo

  8. Hydropower dependence as vulnerability

    hydro share 55% (avg 68%); 2021 EAR trough 24%; wind+solar now 34%

  9. The demographic window is closing

    TFR 2022=1.63 (crossed below 2.1 replacement in 2003)

  10. Resource determinism vs institutions (investment ceiling)

    GFCF/GDP 2023=16.5% (vs ~20% target; gap +3.5pts)

  11. Virtual water and the deforestation tradeoff

    Forest area 1990=5,888,980 -> 2022=4,941,960 km2 (-16.1%)

Sources & method

How the verdicts work

Thresholds are fixed in code (hypotheses.py) before the data is read, and every build re-runs every test, so a verdict can change as new data arrives.

Supported: every condition in the threshold is met. Mixed: some conditions are met, or the main statistic is in the agreed middle band. Not supported: the result falls on the refuting side. Insufficient data: an input is missing.

Correlations are not causal estimates. Ownership of power plants is today's snapshot applied to history; operated oil output is gross, not equity; pre-salt share after 2023 extends ANP's own well classification.
