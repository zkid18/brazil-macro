## A1. Unit of analysis and attribution

Legislatures start 1 Feb: 48th 1987, 49th 1991, 50th 1995, 51st 1999, 52nd 2003, 53rd 2007, 54th 2011, 55th 2015, 56th 2019, 57th 2023, 58th 2027. Because most legislatures straddle two presidencies, the analytical unit is the **president × legislature dyad** (14 dyads 1987–2027; 13 historical, min 12 months to enter: Sarney×48 1987-02→1990-03, Collor×48 1990-03→1991-02 (10.5 m, dropped), Collor×49, Itamar×49, FHC×50, FHC×51, Lula×52, Lula×53, Dilma×54, Dilma×55 (2015-02→2016-05, 15 m), Temer×55, Bolsonaro×56, Lula×57, winner×58). Alignment variables are measured at dyad start (seat shares at the legislature's inauguration or the president's inauguration, whichever is later) and at mid-dyad (cabinet reshuffles).

```sql
WITH leg AS (SELECT * FROM (VALUES
  (48, DATE '1987-02-01', DATE '1991-02-01'), (49, DATE '1991-02-01', DATE '1995-02-01'),
  (50, DATE '1995-02-01', DATE '1999-02-01'), (51, DATE '1999-02-01', DATE '2003-02-01'),
  (52, DATE '2003-02-01', DATE '2007-02-01'), (53, DATE '2007-02-01', DATE '2011-02-01'),
  (54, DATE '2011-02-01', DATE '2015-02-01'), (55, DATE '2015-02-01', DATE '2019-02-01'),
  (56, DATE '2019-02-01', DATE '2023-02-01'), (57, DATE '2023-02-01', DATE '2027-02-01'),
  (58, DATE '2027-02-01', DATE '2031-02-01')) AS t(leg_id, start, "end")),
dyad AS (
  SELECT l.leg_id, p.president, p.party, p.lean,
         greatest(l.start, p.start) AS start, least(l."end", p."end") AS "end",
         row_number() OVER (ORDER BY greatest(l.start, p.start)) AS dyad_id
  FROM br.leg l JOIN br.political_terms p ON p.start < l."end" AND p."end" > l.start)
SELECT * FROM dyad WHERE date_diff('month', start, "end") >= 12 ORDER BY start;
```
(`leg` can live in the in-memory DB; drop the `br.` prefix there.) Monthly attribution: `JOIN dyad d ON o.date >= d.start AND o.date < d."end"`; annual: year t → dyad in office on 1 July t; quarterly GDP by `date − 45 days`.
