UNIT. President x legislature dyad (legislatures start 1 Feb: 48th 1987 ... 58th 2027); dyads with >= 12 months enter (Collor x 48 is dropped at 10.5 months). Monthly attribution: date in [start, end). Annual attribution: the dyad in office on 1 July. Quarterly GDP: date - 45 days.

NARRATIVES (both recorded, both reported whatever the outcome).
N1 "Governability": more fragmentation or a smaller presidential bloc leads to a lower success rate, fewer reforms, a worse primary balance, faster debt growth, a higher real rate and risk premium, and lower investment. Budget capture through emendas raises rigidity and crowds out investment. FPA power raises deforestation and EU-access risk.
N2 "Coalitional presidentialism works / Congress as fiscal check" (Figueiredo–Limongi): executive success is about 75% regardless of fragmentation. Reforms pass under weak presidents too (2016, 2019, 2023). Fiscal and market outcomes are driven by terms of trade, fiscal rules and the BCB, not by alignment. Emendas are mostly capex transfers, so GFCF is not lower. Deforestation follows executive enforcement.

HYPOTHESES (explanatory -> outcome; N1 expectation | N2 expectation)
G1 Fragmentation -> fiscal. Explanatory: enpp_cd, frag_cd, pres_party_share_cd, coalition_share_cd_start. Outcomes: primary_balance_gdp (dyad mean), change in gross_public_debt_gdp (end - start, pp per year), interest_bill_gdp, wb/GC.NLD.TOTL.GD.ZS.BR. N1: worse with fragmentation or a smaller bloc | N2: no association after ToT.
G2 Alignment -> real rate / risk premium. Explanatory: as G1 plus pres_congress_distance. Outcomes: real_policy_rate, embi_brazil, gov_real_yield_10y, wb/FR.INR.RISK.BR. N1: lower with alignment | N2: none.
G3 Alignment -> governability. Outcomes: gov_success_rate (win-rate and follow-rate kept separate), mp_conv_rate, vetoes_overridden share. N1: higher with alignment | N2: success about 75% and flat; MP conversion falls after 2019 regardless (rule change).
G4 Alignment -> reform output (economic constitutional amendments and major economic laws per dyad-year). N1: more with alignment | N2: no relation.
G5 Budget capture (emendas share of discretionary spending; emendas % GDP; 2014–2026) -> wb/GC.NFN.TOTL.GD.ZS.BR, wb/NE.GDI.FTOT.ZS.BR, gov_real_yield_10y, r_minus_g. N1: investment down, yields up | N2: GFCF unchanged; yields track r-g. Spearman only (n about 12).
G6 Reform events -> markets. Group "G1-tightening/institutional" vs group "G2-loosening by Congress". N1: tightening -> yields down / BRL up; loosening -> the reverse | N2: both about 0 against the placebo (priced at the committee stage).
   PRIMARY event date = the first Chamber floor vote (first round); SECONDARY = promulgation/enactment. Both reported. ALIGNMENT DEFINITIONS. Aligned dyad = coalition_share_cd_start >= 0.50 of Chamber seats (fallback: president's bloc share by BLS ideology if coalition is missing). Main continuous variables: pres_party_share_cd, coalition_share_cd_start, enpp_cd, pres_congress_distance.

ESTIMATORS. Spearman rho and OLS slope of the dyad statistic on each explanatory variable; ToT-adjusted variant (residual of y on dlogToT and logToT, as in politics Q0); cluster bootstrap over dyads (5,000 draws, seed 0, 80% CI); exact permutation p (shuffling the explanatory variable across dyads, or full enumeration if n <= 8; else 20,000 shuffles) with its floor reported. BH (q = 0.10) and Holm across the primary family (G1, G2, G3 x main explanatory variable).

VERDICT RULE (reused from politics). "Supports Nx" = the baseline sign matches Nx AND >= 80% of robustness cells share that sign AND the 80% cluster-bootstrap CI excludes zero; otherwise "no robust association". The N2 null is supported when the baseline is near zero and the CI straddles zero ("consistent with N2, not proof").

ROBUSTNESS GRID. Sample (1987+ / 1995+ / 2003+) x unit (dyad / year with dyad clusters) x alignment variable (pres_party_share_cd / coalition_share_cd_start / coalition_share_cd_mid / pres_congress_distance / enpp_cd) x centrão coding (narrow / broad, for the centrão-share variant) x ideology source (BLS / Bolognesi, where available) x attribution (contemporaneous / lag-1 / drop-first-year) x adjustment (raw / ToT / ToT + lean). Sign-consistency share per metric.

POWER STATEMENT (verbatim in the report). 13 dyads, of which 6–8 have the rate/risk series; with a binary aligned/misaligned split of 6 vs 7, C(13,6) = 1,716 labelings (min p 0.001), but the explanatory variable is continuous and dyads of the same president are not independent; with the 2000+ sample there are 7 dyads. This is an effect-size study with bands; nothing is expected to clear BH at q = 0.10.
