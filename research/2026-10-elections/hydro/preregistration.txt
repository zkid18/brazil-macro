1. Narratives (both reported regardless of outcome)
F "Fragility": hydro dependence is still a macro risk. Droughts raise power prices, feed administered inflation and
  expectations, force the BCB, cut agro output and raise fire/deforestation and EU-access risk. Load growth tightens the margin.
D "Diversified": wind+solar (~30%), thermal backup and the 2001 lessons make drought a price/inflation shock only.
  Policy can suppress pass-through (2022), yields are irrigation/tech-driven, and EU access is unaffected by hydrology.

2. Definitions fixed now
- Sample (monthly): 2015-01 to the last common month (ONS daily/weekly averaged to calendar months; IPCA to 2026-08).
- Drought shock S_t = max(0, 40 - EAR_t)/10, where EAR_t is the monthly mean of stored_energy_ear. One unit = 10 pts below 40.
- Episode month = a month inside a run of >= 3 consecutive months with monthly-mean EAR < 40.
- Daily episode (episodes.csv) = a run of >= 90 consecutive days with daily EAR < 40. Gaps <= 7 days above 40 are merged.
- CMO deflated: cmo_power_cost monthly mean x (IPCA index 2026-08 / IPCA index month), the index chained from ipca_monthly.
  log CMO uses log(CMO + 10) because 2023 has zero CMO.
- Bootstrap: moving-block, 2,000 draws, seed 0, block length 12 (monthly) or 3 (annual). 90% percentile CIs.
- Local projections (LP): y(t,h) = a + b_h S_t + c1 Brent12_t + c2 ylag_t + e. Brent12 = 100 x 12-month log change of
  brent_usd monthly mean. ylag = the outcome's own trailing 12-month change at t-1. h = 0..12.
  Outcomes: cumulative administered IPCA (sum of monthly pct t..t+h); cumulative headline IPCA; focus_ipca_12m change
  (month mean t+h minus t-1); focus_selic_12m change; selic_target change in bp (month mean t+h minus t-1).
- Verdict labels: "F" (F's prediction met at its threshold), "D", "Both agree", "Partial", "Null/inconclusive".

3. Hypotheses, tests and thresholds
W1 Drought -> power price. Ratio of mean nominal CMO in episode months vs other months, 2015-2026. OLS of log(CMO_real+10)
   on EAR, EAR x WS (wind+solar share), log Brent. Both narratives expect ratio >= 2x ("Both agree" if met). D's addition:
   the EAR x WS coefficient > 0 (slope flattens with WS) with 90% CI > 0 -> D addition supported.
W2 Drought -> inflation. LP b_12 for cumulative administered IPCA. F if b_12 >= +1.0 pp with 90% CI > 0. D if b_12 < +0.5 pp
   (or CI includes 0). Otherwise Partial. Headline reported alongside (F expects ~ 0.25 x admin). Robustness: drop 2015
   (tariff realignment), drop 2022 (election-year cuts), continuous EAR shock (-(EAR-mean)/10).
W3 Drought -> expectations and Selic. LP peak of Focus IPCA 12m change over h = 0..12. F if >= +0.5 pp with CI > 0; Selic
   F if +50 bp or more with CI > 0. D if neither. 2021 decomposition: the administered contribution to the 12-month IPCA
   change Jan->Nov 2021, using an administered weight from an external fact (IBGE/BCB, ~ 25%).
W4 Drought -> output. 2001 descriptive (quarterly GDP YoY path, annual WB GDP growth vs 2000 and 2002). Post-2015: OLS of IBC-Br
   12-month growth on S_t with Brent12 control, 2020-03..2020-12 excluded (COVID), block bootstrap. F if b < 0 with CI
   excluding 0. D if CI includes 0.
W5 Drought -> agro. Annual OLS 1961-2023: 100 x dlog cereal yield on SPEI and SPEI x (year-1990)/10; same for agri VA growth.
   F if b_SPEI > 0 with 90% CI > 0 AND interaction >= 0. D if interaction < 0 (shrinking) or b_SPEI CI includes 0.
   Exports (USD, 2015-2025): descriptive correlation of soy/coffee/sugar export YoY with SPEI and EAR (price offsets volume).
W6 Diversification lowers sensitivity. Mean log real CMO per EAR band (20-30, 30-40, 40-50, 50-60, 60+) in 2015-2021 vs
   2022-2026; bootstrap difference per band. D if lower in every band with data in both periods and CI < 0 in at least
   one band. F if not lower in the 40-50 band. Bands < 40 have no post-2022 months -> declared untestable in advance.
   Also the 2024 episode (EAR min 43.7, CMO max 625) vs 2017-18 months at similar EAR.
W7 Load growth (data-centre check). Annual load YoY (ONS GWmed) 2016-2025 on dCDD and IBC-Br annual growth, fitted on
   2016-2022, residuals for 2023-2025 (2025 uses an IBC-only model because CDD ends 2024). F if mean residual 2023-25
   >= 2 pp/yr. D if |mean residual| < 1 pp. Otherwise Partial. A known measurement caveat is checked externally: any ONS
   change in how "carga" counts distributed micro/mini generation (MMGD) after 2023 that would mechanically raise measured load.
W8 Climate trend. Permutation test (10,000 shuffles, seed 0) for mean and variance of SPEI, heat-35 days and CDD,
   1960-1999 vs 2010-latest. F if p < 0.10 in the drier/hotter direction for >= 2 of 3 series. D if none.
W9 Companies. Descriptive: Axia/Petrobras generation and SIN share in episode vs non-episode months (deviation from the
   same calendar year's mean), and monthly USD total returns regressed on dEAR. No verdict threshold; F/D read from signs.
W10 Drought/fire -> EU access. Descriptive: fire years (2016, 2024) vs wb/AG.LND.FRLS.HA.BR, PFLS and exports_to_eu YoY.
   Scenario input only.
W11 Policy suppression. Administered IPCA per unit of drought shock in 2015 and 2021 vs 2022 (election year, LC 194/2022).
   Annual OLS 2015-2025 of administered sum on mean S and an election-year dummy (2018, 2022). Quantify the fiscal cost
   of 2022 suppression from external facts. Both narratives agree that suppression shifts cost to the budget.

4. Multiple testing and power
Eleven hypotheses, about 140 monthly observations with heavy serial correlation, and one dominant drought regime
(2015-2021). The effective number of independent drought episodes is about 4 (2015, 2017-18, 2019-20, 2021). Results are
effect sizes with bands. BH q = 0.10 over the W1-W8 primary p-values is reported, but no claim relies on it.
